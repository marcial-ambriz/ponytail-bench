# Bug: team got double points for one challenge

**Type:** bug report · **Reported by:** player + organizer · **Severity:** high (scoreboard integrity)

## What happened

During Saturday's practice round team "NullPointers" ended up with 200 points for "Warmup", which is worth 100.
Two of them were screen-sharing and pressed Submit on the correct flag at the same second. Both got "Correct!".
Their team now shows two solves of "Warmup" in its solve list and sits above a team that actually solved more.

## Expected

A challenge counts **once per team** (once per user in user mode), no matter how or when it gets solved.

## Acceptance

- **R2.1** However many members of a team submit the correct flag at the same moment, the team ends with exactly
  one solve of that challenge and gets its points once (team score, scoreboard, team solve list).
- **R2.2** Those simultaneous submissions never fail with a server error; each one is answered either "correct" or
  "already solved", and at least one is "correct". A later re-submission by a teammate still says "already solved".
- **R2.3** It must not be possible to end up with two solves for the same team and challenge by any route, including
  the admin tools for submissions.
- **R2.4** Dynamic-value challenges are affected the same way (their value must decay once per solving team, not
  once per duplicate submission).
