# Bug: challenge board counts solves from our hidden staff team

**Type:** bug report · **Reported by:** organizer · **Severity:** medium

## What happened

We play-test every challenge with a "Staff" team and then hide that team (Admin → Teams → Hidden) before the event
opens. The scoreboard correctly ignores it, and the solver list of each challenge does not show it. But the
challenge board still says "1 Solve" on every challenge before any real team has solved anything. Players keep
asking who solved it. We also banned a cheating team last week and its solves still seem to count on the board.

Event runs in teams mode.

## Expected

Solves by hidden or banned teams (or hidden/banned users in user mode) are never counted in the number of solves
players see.

## Acceptance

- **R4.1** The solve count of a challenge excludes hidden accounts everywhere players see it: the challenge board
  (`GET /api/v1/challenges`), the challenge itself (`GET /api/v1/challenges/<id>`) and the "N Solves" label of the
  challenge window.
- **R4.2** Banned accounts are excluded the same way.
- **R4.3** The count always matches the solver list a player can open for that challenge
  (`GET /api/v1/challenges/<id>/solves`); solves by visible teams are still counted.
- **R4.4** Un-hiding an account makes its solves count again.
