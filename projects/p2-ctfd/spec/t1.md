# Feature: per-team member limits that hold even when many people join at once

**Type:** feature request · **Reported by:** event organizer · **Area:** teams

## Context

We run CTFd in teams mode behind gunicorn with threaded workers. Today there is only one global "Team Size"
setting. For our next event some sponsor teams are allowed 6 people while student teams stay at 4, so we need a
limit per team.

We also got burned at kickoff: a captain posted the invite link in a big chat, ~30 people opened it within a few
seconds, and a team limited to 4 ended up with 7 members. Whatever we build, the limit has to hold when lots of
people join at the same moment.

## Requirements

- **R1.1** Admins can give an individual team its own member limit (`member_limit`):
  - in the admin panel, on the team create and team edit forms (the edit form shows the current value);
  - through the teams API: `POST /api/v1/teams` and `PATCH /api/v1/teams/<team_id>` accept `member_limit`, and
    `GET /api/v1/teams/<team_id>` returns it to admins.
  - A valid limit is a whole number from 1 to 1000. Because the admin forms send numbers as strings, a numeric
    string such as `"6"` is accepted too. `null` (or an empty string) removes the team's own limit.
- **R1.2** The limit that applies to a team is its own `member_limit` when set, otherwise the global Team Size
  setting; with neither, the team has no limit.
- **R1.3** Joining a team with its name and password (`/teams/join`) or with an invite link (`/teams/invite`) is
  refused once the team has reached its limit. A refused user stays without a team and sees an error.
- **R1.4** The limit holds under simultaneous joins: if N people try to join a team with k free seats at the same
  moment, exactly k of them end up on the team (never more, and the free seats do get filled).
- **R1.5** Invalid limits (0, negative, fractional, non-numeric, booleans, more than 1000) are rejected with HTTP 400
  and leave the team unchanged.
- **R1.6** Only admins can set or change a team's limit. A captain editing their own team
  (`PATCH /api/v1/teams/me`) cannot change it.
- **R1.7** Lowering a limit below the current number of members never removes anyone; it only stops new joins.
- **R1.8** Whenever a limit applies, the team's public page (`/teams/<team_id>`) and the team's own page (`/team`)
  show the seat usage as `<members> / <limit> members`, e.g. `3 / 4 members`.

## Out of scope

Admins adding a user to a team directly from the admin panel may still go over the limit (intentional override).
